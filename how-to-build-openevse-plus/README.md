## Step 2 – Solder header pins

<p>
  <a href="images/step-02-1.jpg"><img src="images/step-02-1.jpg" alt="Step 2 – photo 1" width="32%"></a>
  <a href="images/step-02-2.jpg"><img src="images/step-02-2.jpg" alt="Step 2 – photo 2" width="32%"></a>
  <a href="images/step-02-3.jpg"><img src="images/step-02-3.jpg" alt="Step 2 – photo 3" width="32%"></a>
</p>

- ⚫ Break header pins to match the holes and solder into place.
- ⚠️ Take care not to touch other components in the area with your soldering iron.
- ⚫ Optional - If you are measuring current with the [CR Magnetics 8450-1000](http://store.openevse.com/products/current-measurement-transformer-kit), you will need an additional 2-pin header for the "A_CT" connection.

## Step 3 – Solder DC/DC converter module

<p>
  <a href="images/step-03-1.jpg"><img src="images/step-03-1.jpg" alt="Step 3 – photo 1" width="32%"></a>
  <a href="images/step-03-2.jpg"><img src="images/step-03-2.jpg" alt="Step 3 – photo 2" width="32%"></a>
  <a href="images/step-03-3.jpg"><img src="images/step-03-3.jpg" alt="Step 3 – photo 3" width="32%"></a>
</p>

- ⚫ Solder the DC to DC converter to the OpenEVSE board.
- ⚠️ Take care not to touch other components with your soldering iron.
- ⚫ Trim the excess leads off the DC to DC converter.

## Step 4 – Power Supply

<p>
  <a href="images/step-04-1.jpg"><img src="images/step-04-1.jpg" alt="Step 4 – photo 1" width="32%"></a>
  <a href="images/step-04-2.jpg"><img src="images/step-04-2.jpg" alt="Step 4 – photo 2" width="32%"></a>
  <a href="images/step-04-3.jpg"><img src="images/step-04-3.jpg" alt="Step 4 – photo 3" width="32%"></a>
</p>

- ⚫ Solder the AC to DC converter to the OpenEVSE board.
- ⚠️ Take care not to touch other components while you solder the power supply leads.
- ⚫ Trim any excessively long leads from the bottom of the board.
