## Step 1 – Remove lid

<p>
  <a href="images/step-01-1.jpg"><img src="images/step-01-1.jpg" alt="Step 1 – photo 1" width="32%"></a>
  <a href="images/step-01-2.jpg"><img src="images/step-01-2.jpg" alt="Step 1 – photo 2" width="32%"></a>
</p>

- ℹ️ Unplug your station or turn off circuit breaker. Never work on your station energized.
- 🔴 Remove the 4 Torx T10 Screws.
- ⚠️ Carefully lift the lid and disconnect the ribbon cable for the indicator lights.

---

## Step 2 – Remove Juicebox Controller Board

<p>
  <a href="images/step-02-1.jpg"><img src="images/step-02-1.jpg" alt="Step 2 – photo 1" width="32%"></a>
  <a href="images/step-02-2.jpg"><img src="images/step-02-2.jpg" alt="Step 2 – photo 2" width="32%"></a>
</p>

- 🔴 Remove the 4 11/32" nuts on each corner of the controller board.
- 🟠 Remove all of the connectors. Note most have tabs which when pressed release the connector.
- ⚫ Remove the circuit board.

---

## Step 3 – Install OpenEVSE controller

<p>
  <a href="images/step-03-1.jpg"><img src="images/step-03-1.jpg" alt="Step 3 – photo 1" width="32%"></a>
</p>

- ⚫ Install the OpenEVSE controller board.
- 🔴 Secure the controller with the 4 11/32" nuts.

---

## Step 4 – Connect AC wires

<p>
  <a href="images/step-04-1.jpg"><img src="images/step-04-1.jpg" alt="Step 4 – photo 1" width="32%"></a>
  <a href="images/step-04-2.jpg"><img src="images/step-04-2.jpg" alt="Step 4 – photo 2" width="32%"></a>
</p>

- 🔴 Identify the AC wires coming from the circuit board/Relay Module. Tie wrap them together.
- 🟠 Connect AC_INPUT (RED and BLACK) and AC_TEST (BLUE and ORANGE)

---

## Step 5 – Connect Low Voltage wires

<p>
  <a href="images/step-05-1.jpg"><img src="images/step-05-1.jpg" alt="Step 5 – photo 1" width="32%"></a>
  <a href="images/step-05-2.jpg"><img src="images/step-05-2.jpg" alt="Step 5 – photo 2" width="32%"></a>
  <a href="images/step-05-3.jpg"><img src="images/step-05-3.jpg" alt="Step 5 – photo 3" width="32%"></a>
</p>

- 🔴 Connect Antenna wire to the WiFi module
- 🟠 Connect the donut coil WITHOUT the additional wire wrap to the AMP_CT connector. One Power wire runs through this coil.

---

## Step 6 – Connect more low voltage wires

<p>
  <a href="images/step-06-1.jpg"><img src="images/step-06-1.jpg" alt="Step 6 – photo 1" width="32%"></a>
  <a href="images/step-06-2.jpg"><img src="images/step-06-2.jpg" alt="Step 6 – photo 2" width="32%"></a>
  <a href="images/step-06-3.jpg"><img src="images/step-06-3.jpg" alt="Step 6 – photo 3" width="32%"></a>
</p>

- 🔴 Connect the 3 pin connector that goes to the relay and has the pilot line to the EV handle to the PILOT - 12vDC relay connector.
- 🟠 Connect the donut coil WITH the additional wire wrap to the GFCI connector. Connect the wrap to the Test Coil Connector.

---

## Step 7 – Install Lid

<p>
  <a href="images/step-07-1.jpg"><img src="images/step-07-1.jpg" alt="Step 7 – photo 1" width="32%"></a>
  <a href="images/step-07-2.jpg"><img src="images/step-07-2.jpg" alt="Step 7 – photo 2" width="32%"></a>
</p>

- 🔴 Connect the ribbon cable for the indicator lights.
- 🟠 Secure the 4 TORX T10 screws on each corner of the lid.
