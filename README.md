# OpenEVSE Guides

Assembly and setup guides for OpenEVSE charging stations, written in Markdown.

Each guide has its own folder with a `README.md` and an `images/` directory.

## Guides

| Guide | Difficulty | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Current - v6.5.1 OpenEVSE Advanced](v6.5.1-openevse-advanced/README.md) | Moderate | 30 min – 2 hr |

> [!WARNING]
> OpenEVSE assembly involves AC voltages from 100 to 250 V. If you are not experienced with AC wiring, have an electrician assist with and inspect your work.
